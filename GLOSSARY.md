# Workshop Tools

Live, browser-based activities a Facilitator runs with a team during workshops.

## People

**Facilitator**:
The person who creates a Room and controls its Activity (opening rounds, revealing).
_Avoid_: Manager, host, admin, owner

**Participant**:
A person who has joined a Room under a display name.
_Avoid_: User, member, attendee, voter

## Rooms

**Room**:
A temporary shared space, identified by a URL, running exactly one Activity. It exists only while its Facilitator is present.
_Avoid_: Session, lobby, channel

**Activity**:
The kind of exercise a Room runs: Fist of Five or Word Cloud.
_Avoid_: Tool, game, mode

**Reveal**:
The moment the Facilitator makes a round's hidden answers visible to everyone. After it, the round's answers are locked.
_Avoid_: Show, publish, unveil

## Fist of Five

**Vote**:
One round of Fist of Five: an optional question that each Participant answers with a value from 1 to 5. Only one Vote is open at a time.
_Avoid_: Poll, round, question

**Hand**:
A Participant's answer to a Vote, shown as a hand with that many fingers.
_Avoid_: Score, rating, ballot

## Word Cloud

**Prompt**:
The optional question that starts a Word Cloud round.
_Avoid_: Topic, question

**Entry**:
A short phrase (up to about three words) a Participant submits to the round. Entries that match after ignoring case, accents and surrounding spaces are the same Entry.
_Avoid_: Word, tag, answer

**Cloud**:
The revealed set of Entries, each sized by how many times it was submitted.
_Avoid_: Results, board
